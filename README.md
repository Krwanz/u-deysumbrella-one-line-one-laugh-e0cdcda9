## The entry

> My smart fridge emailed me a diet plan, so I unplugged it, ate the evidence, and blamed the dog.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The output is a single line of 96 characters, within the 140-character limit.

## Why this line

The joke turns a household appliance into a nagging health coach: a fridge that emails you a diet plan has crossed a line, so the owner answers with a three-beat escalation — unplug the snitch, eat the evidence, and blame the dog. Each beat is a small crime, and the last one is the punchline: the dog takes the fall for a diet the fridge invented. No setup paragraph, no meme, no explanation — the whole laugh lands in one breath.
